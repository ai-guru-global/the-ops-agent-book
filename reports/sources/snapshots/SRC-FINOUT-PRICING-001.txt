FAQs
01
How Does Finout Pricing Work?
Finout charges a flat annual fee based on your committed cloud and AI spend tier. It’s not priced per seat, and it’s not a percentage of your bill, so your fee doesn’t change when monthly usage goes up or down.
02
Why Isn’t Pricing Listed on This Page?
Two companies with the same cloud spend can have very different environments: number of cost centers, data volume, and add-ons such as Kubernetes. A single list price would overcharge some teams and undercharge others, so we quote against your actual environment instead.
03
Does Finout Charge a Percentage of My Cloud Bill?
No. Many FinOps tools charge a percentage of the cloud spend they manage, so their fee grows with your bill. Finout’s fee is fixed for the contract term, so saving money or growing your infrastructure doesn’t change what you pay.
04
Is Finout Priced per User?
No. Pricing isn’t based on seats, so engineering, finance and leadership can all use Finout without adding cost per person.
05
Is Kubernetes and AI Spend Included in the Price?
AI spend is part of your subscription. Costs from providers like OpenAI, Anthropic and Cursor are tracked in the same MegaBill as your cloud spend, with no separate AI license. Kubernetes cost allocation is available as an add-on, and it’s priced into your flat fee upfront, so there are no surprise charges later. Your quote will show exactly what’s included.
06
