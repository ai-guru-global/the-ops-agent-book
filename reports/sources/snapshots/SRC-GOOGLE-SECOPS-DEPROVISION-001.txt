Deleting a Google SecOps instance does not cancel your billing. If you have an active contract, you're still responsible for the full contract value, even after you delete the instance. Billing continues until the contract term ends.

Self-service deletion has a 12-day soft delete grace period followed by hard delete. During soft delete, UI/API access is disabled and ingestion is halted; the tenant can be restored.

Projects where customers directly manage their own resources and roles aren't deleted. Customers are solely responsible for deleting these projects and associated data.

After the soft delete phase, data deletion starts. All customer data, including backup snapshots, are permanently deleted. The deletion duration depends on factors including regional laws and contracts.

Associated VirusTotal and Mandiant instances require manual deprovisioning.
