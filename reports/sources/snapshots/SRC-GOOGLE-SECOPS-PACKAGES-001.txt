Google Security Operations is offered in three packages: Standard, Enterprise, and Enterprise Plus.

Standard includes core capabilities for data ingestion, threat detection, investigation, and response. It provides 12 months of hot data retention.

Enterprise includes Standard plus enhanced threat intelligence, UEBA, and Google Cloud's generative AI assistance.

Enterprise Plus includes Enterprise plus Google Threat Intelligence Enterprise Plus, advanced data pipeline management, and extended storage options.

The pricing for all packages is based on ingestion volume. For more information, contact a sales representative or your Google Cloud partner.
