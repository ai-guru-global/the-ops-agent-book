The customer data center option can deliver SAP Business AI capabilities such as Joule within the customer's infrastructure.

The offering includes embedded ERP AI use cases running locally and necessary AI Foundation services accessed through a secure connection to SAP Business Technology Platform cloud infrastructure.

SAP states that payload data never leaves the customer's physical landscape to satisfy national security and residency mandates.
