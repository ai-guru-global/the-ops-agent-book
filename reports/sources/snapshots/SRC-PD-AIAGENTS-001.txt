Questions? We have answers
What is Agentic AI? How does it work?
Agentic AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that can act autonomously to achieve specified goals, make decisions, and take actions in an environment with minimal human intervention. Learn more
here
.
What are the benefits of AI Agents?
AI Agents are a powerful tool to assist modern businesses with resource constraints, boosting productivity and efficiency. AI Agents can reduce operating costs by improving operational maturity, mitigating the risk of human error and operational failure to deliver better customer experiences, and accelerate the pace of innovation to drive growth.
What AI agents does PagerDuty offer?
Paige, PagerDuty's SRE Agent
, is your first line of defense across every step of the incident: an agentic on-call teammate that takes the page, mobilizes the right people and agents, and runs the fix with your approval, offloading work from your team. Paige doesn’t work alone, coordinating specialized agents that handle the work around every incident:
Scribe Agent
captures key moments from incident response and coordinates comms so nothing gets lost between shifts or tools.
Shift Agent
keeps on-call schedules and escalation paths running so the right person — or agent — is always reachable.
Insights Agent
surfaces patterns and recommendations drawn from operational history.
How is Paige different from standalone AI SRE tools?
Paige is built into the incident response platform your team already uses, not added alongside it. Paige is already on call when an incident fires, pulls in the right people and agents, and goes beyond diagnosis: Paige runs the fix with your approval and confirms service was restored. Every capability is governed by PagerDuty’s trust layer, built on 17 years of operating the world’s most mission-critical systems.
Can Paige take action in production, and who approves it?
Yes, within guardrails you set. Paige recommends the remediation workflow that best fits the incident, with the reasoning behind the recommendation. Every remediation waits for your approval unless you decide Paige can act independently. Agent profiles and permissions, scoped per team, define exactly which integrations Paige can access and which actions Paige can take.
What Large Language Models (LLMs) does PagerDuty use?
PagerDuty Advance generates outputs with the assistance from certain LLM providers. Please read the
PagerDuty Advance AI Disclosure
to learn more about how we designed, built, and assessed PagerDuty Advance with mission-critical work in mind, including any third party models used.
What do I need to get started?
Paige is available with the PD Reliability Platform or PagerDuty Advance. Setup has three steps: connect your tools, add Paige to an escalation policy or incident workflow, and set your approval rules. For a full walkthrough, see the
Paige docs
.
How is PagerDuty protecting customer data?
PagerDuty follows our published
guidelines for the safe and secure use of Generative AI
. This is part of our broader Security and Privacy by Design discipline and includes protection of customer data in transit and at rest, and controls on who and how we process customer data. We will offer customer choice in the use of Generative AI features, including whether customer data is or may be used for training purposes. Currently no customer data is used for training purposes.
