What is the difference between FinOps and AI and Cloud Costs?
FinOps is a cultural practice and methodology that brings together finance, engineering, and business teams to collaborate on cloud financial management decisions. It's the “how” of managing cloud costs through people, processes, and technology. AI and Cloud Costs refers to the specific tools, platforms, and technologies used to monitor, analyze, and optimize cloud spending. It's the “what”: the actual tooling and capabilities.
