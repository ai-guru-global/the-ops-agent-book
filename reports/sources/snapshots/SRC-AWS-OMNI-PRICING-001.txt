Amazon CloudWatch Omni pricing
Amazon CloudWatch Omni is a unified observability experience for applications and agents. Standard CloudWatch rates apply for ingestion and storage. Pay for what you query above the included allowance.
Amazon CloudWatch Omni
Overview
Pricing
How pricing works
Omni incurs charges based on three dimensions: the telemetry you send to CloudWatch, the data you retain in CloudWatch, and the analysis you run in Omni.
Ingest
Charged per GB ingested. Logs, metrics, traces, and events from any source - application, infrastructure, or third-party.
Store
Charged per GB-month retained. Logs and spans stored in CloudWatch, with optional Intelligent Tiering across three storage tiers.
Analyze
Charged per GB scanned for logs and traces, and per million samples for PromQL queries. Dashboards and alerts are included.

Pricing FAQ
11
Pricing FAQ
Open all
Is Omni pricing in addition to what I already pay for CloudWatch?
CloudWatch Omni uses telemetry you already send to CloudWatch. Your ingestion and storage rates stay the same — they are standard CloudWatch rates. Omni adds a query charge only when you scan logs or traces for more than your included allowance. PromQL queries are charged at standard CloudWatch rates, and agent evaluations are charged at Amazon Bedrock AgentCore rates. Dashboards and Omni Alerts come with Omni at no additional cost, within service quotas.
Does Omni store a second copy of my telemetry?
Omni operates on the same telemetry stored in CloudWatch. You are only charged for CloudWatch storage.
How does the included query allowance work?
Each month, you can scan up to 5× your total eligible log and span ingestion at zero cost. Scanning beyond the allowance is billed at $0.005 per GB.
Eligible ingestion includes:
Application and custom logs
— usage types
DataProcessing-Bytes
,
DataProcessingIA-Bytes
OpenTelemetry logs
— usage types
DataProcessingOtel-Bytes
,
DataProcessingOtelIA-Bytes
Vended logs
— usage types
VendedLog-Bytes
,
VendedLogIA-Bytes
(including CloudFront, WAF, and MediaTailor variants)
Spans
— usage type
Application-Signals-Bytes
All log classes, custom logs, and vended logs count. The allowance is calculated on total combined ingestion per account.
Example:
10 TB of logs and 2 TB of spans in a month yields a 60 TB included query allowance (12 TB × 5). Scanning beyond 60 TB is billed at $0.005/GB.
How do I see my total ingestion to estimate my query allowance?
You can use
AWS Cost Explorer
— filter by service "AmazonCloudWatch" and group by Usage Type. Look for the usage types listed above (
DataProcessing-Bytes
,
VendedLog-Bytes
,
Application-Signals-Bytes
, and their variants).
Does the 5× query allowance apply only to OpenTelemetry ingestion?
All CloudWatch log and span ingestion counts — whether sent via the CloudWatch agent, the SDK, OTLP, or arriving as vended logs from AWS services.
The $1,000 one-time free trial credits apply to OpenTelemetry log ingestion (
DataProcessingOtel-Bytes
,
DataProcessingOtelIA-Bytes
,
DataProcessingOtelEss-Bytes
), OpenTelemetry metrics (
CW:OTEL:Bytes
), and Application Signals spans (
Application-Signals-Bytes
). The 5× query allowance is a separate, ongoing benefit that covers logs and spans but not metrics ingestion.
How does span pricing relate to Application Signals and X-Ray?
Spans are priced per GB at the rates on the pricing page ($0.35–$0.15/GB, tiered under usage type
Application-Signals-Bytes
). This is the same per-GB rate as Application Signals.
The existing per-span indexing charge (
XRay-Spans-Indexed
, per million X-Ray trace summaries) still applies for trace summaries indexed beyond the first 1% free tier. The per-GB price covers ingestion and storage; the per-span charge covers making individual traces searchable by trace ID.
Do dashboards generate query charges?
Dashboards execute queries, and those queries count toward scan volume. The 5× included allowance applies first — most customers' dashboard usage stays well within it.
To manage costs, set dashboard auto-refresh intervals to 5 minutes or longer. The refresh interval is configurable per dashboard.
What happens when free trial credits run out?
Standard metering applies. Omni stays enabled with zero disruption.
Trial details:
$1,000 in free credits, automatically applied when Omni is enabled.
Credits cover OpenTelemetry logs, OpenTelemetry metrics, and Application Signals spans for up to 30 days.
Available to up to 10 accounts per AWS Organization.
The free trial and the 5× query allowance are independent. The 5× allowance is ongoing.
Are log classes (Standard, and Infrequent Access) still supported?
All existing CloudWatch Logs log classes remain supported. Telemetry in every log class is available for Omni querying, dashboards, and alerts. Log class determines the ingestion rate; the Omni query rate ($0.005/GB scanned) is the same across all log classes.
What is included at no additional charge?
Omni Dashboards
— build and share dashboards within service quotas.
Omni Alerts
— create alerts within service quotas. Existing CloudWatch alarms remain separately priced.
Application topology map
— service dependency visualization.
Investigation agent
— AI-powered anomaly triage.
Query within the 5× allowance
— scanning up to 5× monthly ingestion volume.
Your bill scales with data volume — the same rate whether you have ten hosts or ten thousand, one user or a hundred.
All included capabilities are subject to CloudWatch service quotas.
Can I get custom or volume pricing?
Yes. Contact your AWS account team.
