At a glance for EU buyers
EU customers can request an in-region Dropzone AI deployment. No customer data is used to train Dropzone AI's models or its sub-processors' models. Each customer runs in a dedicated tenant with no co-mingling of data.
In-region deployment
EU customers can request an in-region deployment. Each customer runs in a dedicated tenant with no co-mingling, and the context your agents learn about your environment stays in that tenant.
No model training
No customer data is used to train Dropzone AI's models or its sub-processors' models. Security experts employed by Dropzone AI manually review investigations to improve the agents' knowledge and tools.
Evidence-backed audit trail
Each investigation ends in a report with the data sources used and links to the raw evidence. Your analysts review it and own every verdict.

How EU data residency works for AI SOC investigations
Alerts from the sources you connect arrive in your dedicated tenant. The AI SOC Analyst forms hypotheses about why each alert fired and collects evidence from your security tools and internal systems for each line of investigation. Investigations operate within the integrations and permissions you configure, and data is pulled only as needed to resolve the alert in question. This supports the data minimisation principle in GDPR Article 5.

Your EU deployment questions, answered
How does Dropzone AI handle investigation data for EU customers?
EU customers can request an in-region deployment. Each customer runs in a dedicated tenant in its own isolated subnet, with no co-mingling of data.
You choose which alert and data sources connect. Confirm the region and processing locations for your deployment with Dropzone AI when you scope it.
Do the LLMs learn from our data?
No. Customer data is not used to train Dropzone AI's models or its sub-processors' models.
Dropzone AI's LLM providers are contractually obligated not to store or retain your data beyond immediate query processing. Your context memory exists only within your tenant.
Are investigations fully auditable?
Each investigation produces a report with the recommended conclusion, the data sources used and links to the raw evidence.
Data lineage provides an audit trail for the analysis. Investigation data is retained until contract end or on request.
Are humans in control?
Yes. Your analysts own every verdict, and each investigation arrives with its evidence for review.
Access is read-only by default, and you choose where to grant write access.
Response actions, such as blocking a malicious IP or disabling a compromised account, run within the bounds you set, on threats the agents have confirmed.
Can humans outside the EU access our data?
Your own team reaches the platform through the roles you assign, with local login, Microsoft or Google single sign-on, or SAML.
You can bring your own encryption keys.
Does any of our data leave the EU?
Investigation data, findings and context memory are stored in your dedicated tenant, which is not shared with other customers. EU customers can request that tenant in-region.
Investigations also call outside services, including LLM providers and threat intelligence feeds. Some feeds may not offer EU-specific endpoints. You decide which alert and data sources connect.
