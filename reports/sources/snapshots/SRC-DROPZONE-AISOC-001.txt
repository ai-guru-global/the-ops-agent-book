Everything an investigation needs,
built in
AI SOC Analyst brings expert-level investigation to every alert, helping your team move from signal to decision faster. It shows its reasoning, learns your environment, works across your security stack, and can act when a threat is confirmed.
It arrives with evidence
What reaches you is already assembled. Evidence gathered across your stack, correlated into a single chain, laid out as a timeline, with a verdict and the reasoning that produced it. The first thing you do with it is decide.
Coverage on every alert
Volume stops deciding what gets looked at. Every alert gets a full investigation and a verdict, malicious, benign, or needs more data, with the evidence behind the call. The ones nobody had hours for get the same treatment as the rest.
Context that persists
Analysts tell it what’s normal here: which host belongs to whom, what changed last quarter, which alerts this environment always throws. That context shapes the next investigation, so what one analyst knows becomes what the whole team works from.
The lead gets followed
Ask a follow-up and it reads the case: the evidence already gathered, the tools it can reach in your tenant. You watch each step as it runs, and you can stop it. The next answer you need does not become its own ticket.
Set what it can act on
You decide what it acts on by itself: blocking an IP, disabling an account. You decide where it stops and what always comes to a person, and you widen that as the evidence earns it.
