Workday Illuminate™ Expands with New AI Agents for HR, Finance, and Industry

Workday, Inc. today announced new Workday Illuminate™ agents for HR, Finance, and Industry. Illuminate agents are purpose-built for work – embedded in HR and Finance, powered by Workday's unmatched data and context, and designed to deliver measurable outcomes. The new agents take on some of the most complex processes – like performance reviews, workforce planning, and financial close – helping organizations work smarter, faster, and with greater confidence.

AI Agents That Deliver Real Business Value

Illuminate agents transform processes like hiring, performance, planning, and financial close into more efficient, strategic outcomes. Designed to work alongside people, these agents handle repetitive, time-consuming tasks so employees can focus on higher-value work.

Workday Flex Credits: A Smarter, More Flexible Way to Access AI Innovation

To give organizations maximum agility in adopting these innovations, Workday is introducing Workday Flex Credits – a new, transparent subscription-based consumption model designed to make AI simple, flexible, and scalable. Flex Credits are included in every subscription, can be applied universally across agents and platform innovations, and grow as customers' needs evolve.

Built-in access - Customers receive an initial allotment of Flex Credits as part of their Workday subscription, renewed annually.
Scalable growth - Organizations can add more credits as their AI usage expands – with no complicated tiers or hidden fees.
Universal flexibility - Flex Credits are fully fungible, enabling customers to apply them across Workday agents and platform innovations as business needs change.

Availability

New Illuminate agents will be available in 2026. Workday Flex Credits are available to purchase starting today.
