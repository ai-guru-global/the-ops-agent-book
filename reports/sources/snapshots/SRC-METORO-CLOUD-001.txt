How does Metoro Cloud handle data retention and storage?
Metoro Cloud offers flexible retention policies from 7 days to 2 years depending on your plan. Data is stored in highly available, geographically distributed storage with automatic backups, and you can export your data at any time through our APIs.

When should I pick self-hosted Metoro instead?
Self-hosted Metoro makes sense if you need data to never leave your VPC, or if you have very specific compliance requirements that prevent using a managed service. Most teams pick Cloud because it removes the operational burden - but the same product is available on-prem.
