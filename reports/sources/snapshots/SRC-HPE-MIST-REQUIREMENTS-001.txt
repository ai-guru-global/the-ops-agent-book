Requirements

Your access depends on your role in the HPE Mist™ management portal and the subscriptions that you've activated for your organization.

User Role
The following user roles can access monitoring information in the management portal:
Super User
Network Admin
Observer
Helpdesk
Super Observer

Subscriptions
Your subscriptions determine the features that are available to you in the management portal.

Base Subscription—With the base subscription, you can:
View AI-native insights and easy-to-interpret graphs for site events, client events, AP events, and more.
Configure alerts to get notified when events happen in your HPE Mist organization.

With a subscription for Wireless Assurance, Wired Assurance, or WAN Assurance, you can monitor service levels and investigate issues impacting user experiences.

HPE Marvis Virtual Network Assistant Subscription—With an HPE Marvis Virtual Network Assistant subscription, you can:
Chat with your conversational network assistant to ask questions and troubleshoot issues.
Submit structured queries using HPE Marvis Query Language.
View the HPE Marvis Actions page, which identifies issues, presents a root cause analysis, and recommends actions.
Use the HPE Marvis Windows and Android client.
Integrate HPE Mist with apps such as Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and more.
