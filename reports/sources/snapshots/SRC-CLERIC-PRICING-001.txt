Connect your repos and monitoring and see what Cleric catches. - Start free trial: https://cleric.ai/start - Book a demo: https://cleric.ai/demo ## Frequently asked questions ### What is change verification? Each release is split into production changes, usually one per pull request. For up to 14 days after a change deploys, Cleric watches for regressions, and if one shows up you get the cause and a proposed fix while the change is still fresh. **Each change costs 2 credits.** ### Which changes does Cleric verify? By default, Cleric follows every change from release messages in Slack or Microsoft Teams. You can add filters if you only want it to verify certain repositories, services, or changes. ### What if a change looks fine and breaks later? Cleric monitors the change for up to 14 days and reports a regression if it detects one. ### What counts as an Issue? An Issue is a production problem Cleric investigates end to end. It can start from a page, scheduled monitor, or failed deploy. Repeat alerts join the open Issue at no extra charge. **Each Issue costs 10 credits.** Follow-up passes are included. ### What does Cleric do for free? Repeat alerts that join an open Issue, retries, and corrections or extra passes on the same Issue are free. A chat request under 30 seconds is free. ### What if Cleric gets it wrong? Corrections and additional passes on the same Issue do not use more credits. ### What happens if I run out of credits? Monthly credits roll over for one month, up to twice your monthly allocation. Annual plans use one annual pool. When credits run out, new work pauses until your next credit release, unless you buy more credits or turn on overage. ### Do you train on our data? No. We do not use customer data to train Cleric or its underlying models. See the [security and privacy policy](https://docs.cleric.ai/security) for how your data is handled and deleted. ### Where is my data hosted? You choose a US or EU hosting region during setup. See [EU hosting and data processing](https://docs.cleric.ai/security/eu-hosting-data-processing) for what runs where. ### How are Chats billed? A Chat is any other agent session that uses Cleric’s knowledge and integrations, including any custom agent invocations you define. A request under 30 seconds is free. After that, each started minute the agent runs costs one credit. Idle time is not billed. ### How does the product evaluation work? The evaluation includes 1,000 credits after approval and has no time limit. Connect Cleric to your code and production tools, then use the credits at your own pace until they run out. ### What does annual billing add? The credit price stays at $0.50. Your credits come as one pool for the year instead of monthly allocations. Enterprise adds an Order Form and MSA, invoice billing, a negotiated DPA, and vendor security reviews. ### What happens at the end of an annual plan? Annual plans run for 12 months and renew automatically. We remind you 30 days before renewal, and you can cancel any time before then. ### Can I buy through AWS Marketplace? Yes. Annual Team, Pro, and Scale plans are available on [AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-lcjp6yga3bpp4) at the same prices, billed through your AWS account. For Enterprise or custom terms, [contact us](/contact) about a private offer.
