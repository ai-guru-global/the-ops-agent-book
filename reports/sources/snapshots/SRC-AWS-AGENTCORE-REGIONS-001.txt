us-east-1, us-east-2, us-west-2, ap-southeast-1, ap-southeast-2, ap-south-1, ap-northeast-1, eu-west-1, eu-central-1.

Gateway data plane endpoints are additionally listed for:
us-west-1, eu-west-2, eu-west-3, eu-south-1, eu-south-2, eu-north-1, ap-northeast-2, ap-south-2, ap-southeast-5, ap-southeast-7, ca-central-1, sa-east-1.

Feature availability must be checked per service; endpoint presence does not prove every AgentCore capability is available in every Region.
