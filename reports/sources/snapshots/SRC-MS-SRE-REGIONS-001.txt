Supported Regions for Azure SRE Agent

Azure SRE Agent is available in select Azure regions.

Available regions
Australia East (australiaeast)
Brazil South (brazilsouth)
Canada Central (canadacentral)
Central India (centralindia)
Central US (centralus)
East Asia (eastasia)
East US 2 (eastus2)
France Central (francecentral)
Italy North (italynorth)
Japan East (japaneast)
Japan West (japanwest)
Korea Central (koreacentral)
North Central US (northcentralus)
South Africa North (southafricanorth)
South India (southindia)
Southeast Asia (southeastasia)
Spain Central (spaincentral)
Sweden Central (swedencentral)
UK South (uksouth)
West Central US (westcentralus)
West US 2 (westus2)
West US 3 (westus3)

If the Region dropdown is empty, you're not registered for Azure SRE Agent. Submit a registration request with your subscription ID.

Each agent is deployed to a single region. To operate in multiple regions, create a separate agent in each region. You can't change an agent's region after creation.
