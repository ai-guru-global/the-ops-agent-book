Assist consumption pricing is based on number of actions that are run, not including the orchestrator or communicator agent actions. AI Agents are bucketed into Small, Medium, and Large.

AI Agents uses OpenAI's GPT-4.1 by default for its orchestration layer on ServiceNow managed Azure servers. AI Agent tools may use Now LLM.

As of July 2025, AI Agents is supported for GCC/self-hosted environments with NowLLM only.
