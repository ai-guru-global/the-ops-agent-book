知识源：Runbooks（monitor message 内 instructions 或 Confluence 链接）、bits.md、Feedback and memories。

Automatic memories（逐字）：
"Bits Investigation automatically stores the root cause and learnings from past investigations, then retrieves them the next time the same monitor alerts."

Feedback memories（逐字）：
"All positive feedback, as well as any negative feedback that includes details provided in the Bits' chat, creates a memory. Bits dynamically selects which memories to use in future investigations to improve its performance."

Memories 管理（逐字）：
"To manage memories, including viewing and deleting them, go to the Memories column of the Monitor Management page."

bits.md 在 Bits Investigation > Settings > Bits.md 创建，可选择作用于 Investigations/Chat；Runbooks 链接由用户定义，Bits 只访问其有权限的链接。
