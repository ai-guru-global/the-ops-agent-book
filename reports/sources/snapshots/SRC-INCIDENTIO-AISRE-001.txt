AI SRE that investigates incidents like your most tenured engineer
Investigations starts debugging the moment you declare an incident, connecting telemetry, code changes, and incident history to surface the root cause in minutes.

Meet Investigations, our always-on AI SRE product.

Root cause in seconds, not minutes
Investigations connects telemetry, code changes, and past incidents to name what broke and why, with a confidence score and sources behind every finding. Your team arrives with a hypothesis, not a blank page.
Humans and agent, working in lockstep
Collaborate with the AI SRE agent across the whole investigation. Ask it questions, have it run parallel analysis, and let it surface things you haven't thought to look for yet.
Respond at a fundamentally different speed
The time between alert and root cause is where incidents drag on and customer impact compounds. Investigations closes that gap so your engineers spend their time fixing, not searching.
Built on the power of Nexus
Investigations runs on Nexus, our production intelligence model. It reasons across your entire environment in real time, so at 3am it can make high-conviction hypotheses on a system it knows inside out.

AI that is trusted when it matters most

Measurably accurate

Evidence-backed audit trail

Your data stays yours

SOC 2 compliant
