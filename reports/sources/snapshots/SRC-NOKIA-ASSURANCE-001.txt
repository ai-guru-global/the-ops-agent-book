What is Nokia Assurance Center?

Nokia Assurance Center is an AI-driven network and service assurance solution for multi-vendor, multi-domain and multi-technology networks.

It ingests and correlates multiple types of data including network, service, subscriber and security data to trigger autonomous operations processes. Its AIOps-based detection and correlation capabilities are augmented by agentic AI for complex reasoning and closed-loop operation.

By predicting and preventing issues before they affect customers and by accelerating problem resolution, Assurance Center significantly improves service quality, operational efficiency and customer experience.

As part of Nokia’s Autonomous Networks portfolio, Assurance Center helps telcos and mission-critical enterprises around the world to evolve faster towards highly autonomous networks (AN Level 4) and beyond.

What are key features of Assurance Center?

360º observability

A single pane of glass enables end-to-end monitoring and management of multi-vendor, multi-technology networks, including 5G, fixed and cloud domains.

AIOps and Agentic Ops

AIOps detects and correlates at scale while agentic AI handles complexity on demand to enable L4+ autonomy.

Scalability and Openness

Designed for deployments of any size, Assurance Center offers open, standards-based APIs for faster and simplified integration.

Service impact analysis (SIA)

Assurance Center determines the impact of network issues on customer services, enabling prioritized responses.

Root cause analysis (RCA)

Leveraging AI/ML capabilities, Assurance Center performs advanced RCA to quickly identify the underlying cause of issues.

Predictive analytics

Assurance Center uses machine learning to predict potential issues before they affect services, enabling proactive maintenance and prevention.

Fault management

Automated detection, correlation and resolution of faults reduce manual effort and accelerate problem-solving.

Performance monitoring

Continuous monitoring of network and service performance provides real-time insights into key performance indicators (KPIs) and service level agreements (SLAs).

Closed-loop automation

Assurance Center leverages assurance insights to close the loop with network operations for automated remediation and optimization.
