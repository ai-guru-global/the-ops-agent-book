Pay-as-you-go usage above quota is available only when the project has at least one active, non-free-trial subscription. Self-serve accounts use prepaid credits; invoiced accounts are billed monthly.

The Pay-as-you-go edition has no subscription or license fee and no feature quota limits. Storage and data indexing are listed at $5 per GiB per month.

Usage above storage and data indexing quota automatically incurs pay-as-you-go charges. Feature overage is optional per edition. Single-user quota limits do not restrict pay-as-you-go usage above the pooled quota.
