Frequently asked questions
Everything you need to know about AI SRE.
What is an AI SRE?
An AI SRE is an AI system that performs the investigation, triage, and coordination tasks traditionally handled by a site reliability engineer during an incident. It monitors alerts, runs root cause analysis, surfaces findings, and assists with resolution—working alongside human engineers rather than replacing them. Much of this work also goes by
AI incident management
, which applies AI across every stage of the incident lifecycle. AIOps tools work earlier in the event stream, cutting noise and correlating alerts, and
AI SRE vs AIOps
explains how the two fit together.
What is Rootly AI SRE?
Rootly AI SRE is an AI-powered investigation and response engine built directly into Rootly's incident management platform. It activates the moment an alert fires, runs parallel hypothesis checks, surfaces root cause with confidence scores, and assists your team through resolution—without replacing human judgment.
How does AI root cause analysis work?
Rootly AI SRE runs parallel hypothesis checks across your alerts, telemetry, recent deployments, and past incidents—building a ranked, evidence-backed theory of what went wrong. Each finding comes with a confidence score and a visible reasoning chain so engineers can evaluate the output before acting on it.
How is Rootly AI SRE different from incident.io's or Resolve AI's?
Rootly AI SRE is built into a complete incident management platform, not layered on top of one. It has full context across your services, team ownership, on-call schedules, and incident history before it starts investigating. It also shows its reasoning at every step, enforces zero third-party training on your data, and never auto-remediates without human sign-off.
Does Rootly train on my incident data?
No. Rootly enforces zero third-party model training. Your incident data is used exclusively for your organization; never pooled with other customers, never used to train general models.
What does “human in the loop” mean in practice?
Rootly AI SRE investigates, surfaces findings, suggests remediation steps, drafts updates and fixes but every change requires explicit human sign-off before execution. Engineers stay in control at every decision point.
How does Rootly AI SRE connect to the rest of the platform?
Rootly AI SRE is natively integrated with Rootly On-Call, Incident Response, Catalog, and has read access your code repositories. It knows which services are affected, who owns them, what's changed recently, and what happened in similar past incidents, without any manual setup or context-switching.
How long does it take to see value?
Teams see Rootly AI SRE surface its first root cause finding within the first incident after setup. Because it's integrated with your existing Rootly platform there's no separate onboarding required.
Does Rootly correlate deploys with incidents?
Yes, Rootly's AI SRE correlates telemetry with recent deploys, commits, and feature-flag changes, plus past incidents, then shows an evidence chain with confidence scores before recommending a fix.
