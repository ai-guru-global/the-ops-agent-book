License Options for Microsoft Copilot

Microsoft Copilot is available with Microsoft 365 E7 or as an add-on for eligible commercial, US Government, and education subscription plans.

Licensing availability and product terms can change. Before you purchase or assign licenses, confirm eligibility in the Microsoft Product Terms, Microsoft Copilot service description, and Microsoft Copilot pricing and plans.

Microsoft Copilot Business is an add-on for organizations with up to 300 users that have eligible Microsoft 365 Business licenses. It's available with an annual commitment and supports up to 300 licensed users per tenant.

Commercial eligible plans include Microsoft 365 E7, E5, E3, F1, F3, Business Premium, Business Standard, Business Basic, Apps for Enterprise and Apps for Business; Office 365 E5, E3, E1 and F3; and eligible Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams, Planner, Project and Visio plans.

US Government
Microsoft Copilot is available as an add-on in Government Community Cloud (GCC), Government Community Cloud High (GCC High), and Department of Defense (DoD) environments for the listed eligible plans.
