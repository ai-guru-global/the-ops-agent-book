Solution Overview
UniSeer Intelligent O&M Solution
ZTE UniSeer intelligent O&M solution provides integrated O&M services oriented to networks, services and user experience. By big data and AI technology, reconstructing the original organization, processes, and tools, and integrating the process of O&M center,Customer Service Center and Marketing Center, realize the transformation from network-centric O&M to customer Customer experience-centered O&M.
Scenario
Intelligent Network Operation
Using AI algorithms, to identify potential faults and discover the root cause relationship between different alarms through learning the historical data，and automatic diagnosis and recovery through automatic scripts. As a result, reduce the number of invalid work orders, improve O&M efficiency and reduce the waste of resources.
Service Quality Assurance
Monitors the indexes of 5 types of services (web/video/game/OTT voice/OTT video). When the indexes degrade beyond the set threshold, it sends alarm to the FMS, automatically triggers the quality analysis and optimization of the original services, and then outputs the analysis report.
End-to-end User Complaint Handling
Perform end-to-end automatic analysis and demarcation of the services during the complaint period. The entire process from data preparation to problem delimitation output can be completed within 30 minutes.
Precise Planning
Based on AI algorithm, it can make the network planning more in line with the actual business development needs, make the investment more accurate and maximize the benefits. It can significantly improve 5G planning efficiency and effectively realize 4G / 5G collaboration.
Operation Support
ZTE creatively proposes a cost model based on site asset information and O&M data, which can calculate the costs to per service/user/GB, helping operators identify high-value services/sites, and providing a reference for network investment and market decision-making.
Advantages and Highlights
Prediction and Prevention System
A comprehensive prediction and prevention system is established from the NE layer, network layer, to the service layer, transform the O&M from passive response to active maintenance
Intelligent RCA
Alarm rules are generated automatically to effectively reduce the number of invalid work orders and improve O&M efficiency
Automatic Diagnosis and Recovery
Quickly locate and restore faults through automatic diagnosis and recovery scripts
Fast Demarcation
The indicator system covers the mainstream services in the entire domain, and delimits users' complaints within 30 minutes
Intelligent Optimization
Automation, intelligence and closed-loop network performance analysis and optimization
Customer Value
Improve the Efficiency of Operation and Maintenance
Promoting Digital Transformation for Operators Based on Big Data and AI Technologies
Reduce Failure Rate
Establish prediction and prevention system to deal with hidden troubles in advance
Leading NOC+SOC Delivery Solution
The first integrated NOC+SOC intelligent O&M delivery solution in the industry, realizing automatic and intelligent network/service/perception management for the operator
Improve ROI
Improve the input-output ratio through Precise planning，cost modeling，precision marketing etc
