HPE Aruba Networking Central
Subscriptions FAQ

What types of subscriptions are available in HPE Aruba Networking Central?

HPE Aruba Networking Central provides two primary subscription tiers: Foundation and Advanced. Additionally, there are subscriptions available for gateways, which include WLAN, SD-Branch, and SD Microbranch options. Bandwidth-based subscriptions can be obtained for third-party devices and are specifically available for federal use (U.S. only). For more details, refer to Aruba Central Ordering Guide https://www.hpe.com/psnow/doc/a00125615enw.

Do I need a Foundation or Advanced subscription in HPE Aruba Networking Central if I have a subscription in HPE Classic Central?

No, the existing Foundation or Advanced subscription in HPE Classic Central is applicable to HPE Aruba Networking Central.

Which type of subscription is required for Copilot and other AI features?

HPE Aruba Networking Central Advanced subscription is required to enable AI features such as Copilot.

To activate Copilot, 100% of all devices in the account must be on an Advanced subscription.

AI Search is available with the Foundation subscription. For a complete breakdown of subscription tiers and features, refer to the Ordering Guide.

Which type of subscription is required for User Experience Insight (UXI)?

For UXI and Central integration, a Foundation subscription is required. UXI hardware requires one, three, or five years subscription.

How can I define Foundation and Advanced subscription features and their dependencies for APs, switches, and gateways added to HPE Aruba Networking Central?

Please refer to our Ordering Guide.
