Available Commercial Regions (OC1)
Brazil East (Sao Paulo), Germany Central (Frankfurt), India South (Hyderabad), Japan Central (Osaka), Saudi Arabia Central (Riyadh), UK South (London), US East (Ashburn), US Midwest (Chicago), US West (Phoenix).

Availability: Not every listed model is available in the preceding listed regions.

External Calls to xAI Grok Models
The xAI Grok models that can be accessed through the OCI Generative AI service in an OCI region are hosted externally by xAI. Therefore, a call to an xAI Grok model through the OCI Generative AI service results in a call to an xAI location.

External Calls to Google Gemini Models
For Google Gemini 2.5 Pro in the Osaka region, requests are routed through a Google Asia-Pacific regional endpoint, but model processing can occur at any Google location globally, including outside Asia-Pacific. Routing through the endpoint does not guarantee Asia-Pacific data residency for processing.

For Gemini 2.5 Flash in the Osaka and Hyderabad regions, requests are routed through a Google Asia-Pacific regional endpoint, but model processing can occur at any Google location globally, including outside Asia-Pacific. Routing through the endpoint does not guarantee Asia-Pacific data residency for processing.
